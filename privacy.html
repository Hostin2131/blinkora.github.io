Privacy Policy

Blinkora Uploader · Effective date: October 11, 2026

What this app is

Blinkora Uploader is a private, internal tool operated by the owner of the Blinkora YouTube channel (@blinkora.shorts). It uses YouTube API Services to upload videos to that channel and to read public statistics (views, likes, comments) of the channel's own videos. It is not offered to the public.

Data we access

  • An OAuth access/refresh token for the channel owner's Google account, granted with the scopes youtube.upload and youtube.readonly.
  • Public video metadata and statistics returned by the YouTube Data API (for example, titles and view counts of public Shorts).

How data is used and stored

The token is stored only on the operator's own server and is used solely to upload the channel's videos and read their statistics. Video statistics are stored locally to decide which content to produce next. We do not sell, rent or share any data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

Third-party services

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and data is handled according to the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at Google security settings – third-party access. On request, all stored tokens and statistics are deleted within 7 days.
